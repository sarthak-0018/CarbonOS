# Assumptions and limitations

- A household electricity activity uses the geography and period attached to its saved activity; frequency is explicit, and monthly kWh is annualized by multiplying by 12.
- Electricity bills are authoritative over appliance estimates. Do not sum overlapping bill and appliance estimates.
- Input quantities require explicit units; only documented conversions are allowed.
- Only unique APPROVED factors with complete provenance are eligible. CO2 and CO2e totals stay separate.
- Partial session totals exclude activities whose factors are unavailable and must never be described as complete.
- Data quality is a transparent source-weighted coverage indicator, not a scientific uncertainty estimate.
- CEA Version 22.0 reports 0.675 tCO2/MWh weighted average for FY 2025-26; this is converted to 0.675 kgCO2/kWh. Dates outside the reference period are labeled with the latest-published-factor policy.
- Petrol/diesel road activity requires user-entered vehicle efficiency; its fuel factors are source-year 2015 defaults. LPG, CNG, food, flights, and public transport remain unavailable until usable factors are loaded.
- Data quality is the source-score arithmetic mean: bills/integration 100, user entered 80, estimated 40. This is not a scientific confidence interval.
